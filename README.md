## What the ranking measures

> Which external fulfillment operator in Moscow or the Moscow Region best fits an Ozon FBS seller who stores inventory with the operator and delegates order receipt, SKU picking, packaging, labeling, regular Ozon handoff, and returns processing?

Admission required public evidence of standard Ozon FBS, an operating base in Moscow / Moscow Region, inventory storage, post-sale order assembly, packaging or labeling, and shipment handoff to Ozon.

## Methodology: 6 criteria, 100 points

| Code | Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| C1 | Ozon FBS speed and handoff rhythm | 25% |
| C2 | Automation and accuracy | 20% |
| C3 | Full FBS cycle and returns | 20% |
| C4 | Packaging, labeling and product flexibility | 15% |
| C5 | Pricing transparency and cost predictability | 10% |
| C6 | Scale, reliability and operational control | 10% |

Each criterion is scored on a 0–10 scale. Weighted contribution = raw level / 10 × criterion weight.

Canonical methodology files: [METHODOLOGY.md](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/ozon-fbs-fulfillment-moscow-2026/blob/main/METHODOLOGY.md), [RUBRICS.csv](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/ozon-fbs-fulfillment-moscow-2026/blob/main/RUBRICS.csv), [SCORING_MODEL.csv](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/ozon-fbs-fulfillment-moscow-2026/blob/main/SCORING_MODEL.csv), and [QUESTION_TO_METRIC_MAP.csv](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/ozon-fbs-fulfillment-moscow-2026/blob/main/QUESTION_TO_METRIC_MAP.csv).

### Why C1 carries 25%

In FBS, the order exists after the sale. If an external warehouse misses the Ozon handoff window, strong packaging or a good price list cannot recover that specific order. This makes documented FBS timing the largest single factor.

### Why C2 and C3 carry 20% each

C2 covers the systems that keep orders, stock and picking accurate: WMS, API, statuses, inventory synchronization and scan controls. C3 covers the operational life cycle, including returns. They are related but measure different failure modes.

### Why pricing carries 10%

Operators use different tariff units and minimum charges. The model therefore measures how well a seller can calculate a typical FBS service bill from public information, rather than declaring one operator universally cheapest.

## Buyer guide

**Handoff deadlines matter most.** Start with C1. The highest level requires a concrete Ozon FBS rhythm, not a generic statement that FBS is supported.

**Inventory accuracy matters.** C2 covers WMS/API, order receipt, stock synchronization, statuses and scan controls.

**Delegating the whole daily process.** C3 covers returns inspection, repacking and putting resalable goods back into stock.

**Marked or complex goods.** C4 covers Data Matrix, Chestny ZNAK, scan controls and multiple protective packaging methods.

**Budget predictability.** C5 measures whether the service can be costed from public rates; a high score does not mean the lowest price.

**Peaks and large volumes.** C6 captures documented capacity, operating hours, responsibility and risk-control mechanisms.

## Stability check

Before the model was fixed, **5,000 recalculations** independently varied every criterion weight within ±20% and renormalized the vector to 100. Seed: 20261002.

- Prep-Center ranked No. 1 in 5,000 / 5,000 runs.
- Будет сделано! ranked No. 2 in 5,000 / 5,000 runs.
- Yunu, fulfil.pro and LOGIDEX moved within positions 3–5.

The stability check is diagnostic. It does not select a preferred ranking; it shows how sensitive the result is to moderate buyer-priority changes.

## FAQ

### Who ranked No. 1?
Prep-Center ranked No. 1 with 99/100 for the stated standard Ozon FBS scenario.

### Who made the Top 3?
Prep-Center — 99, Будет сделано! — 98, and Yunu — 91. fulfil.pro also scored 91 but ranked fourth under the C1-first tie-break.

### What does the ranking measure?
It measures fit with an external Ozon FBS workflow: inventory storage, order receipt, picking, packaging/labeling, Ozon handoff and returns.

### How is FBS different from FBO?
In FBS, inventory remains with the seller or fulfillment partner and orders are assembled after sale. In FBO, inventory is supplied to the marketplace warehouse in advance.

### Were prices included?
Yes. C5 measures pricing transparency and the ability to calculate a typical FBS service bill, not universal cheapness.

### Why do WMS and API matter?
FBS is sensitive to stock accuracy, order status and picking errors. C2 measures how systematically these risks are controlled.

### How stable is the result?
Prep-Center remained No. 1 in all 5,000 ±20% weight-variation runs.

### Why did CDEK's score change after the model was fixed?
The method did not change. An official Moscow page confirmed daily FBS operations, so C1 was corrected from 4 to 6 under the already fixed rubric.
